= U13: Vertex merging and closed profile extraction
:toc:

Issue: https://github.com/Artem535/SketchCAD/issues/60[#60].

User-approved bridge increment between 2D sketching (xref:sketch-fillet.adoc[]) and 3D part extrusion
(xref:roadmap.adoc[], xref:adr-0001-3d-viewer.adoc[ADR-0001]).

== Purpose

Tablet sketching without an exact endpoint snap can leave sub-millimetre gaps between adjacent segments, preventing
3D solid extrusion. Conversely, valid 3D extrusion needs deterministic extraction of closed outer contours and inner
holes (including straight segments, trimmed/filleted arcs, chamfered corners, and full circles) while ignoring
construction geometry such as chamfer/fillet virtual sharps and layout guides.

All geometry analysis and vertex merging live in `sketchcad_core` (`include/sketchcad/sketch_profile.h`,
`src/sketch_profile.cpp`) with zero dependency on Qt or OCCT.

== Part 1: Merge nearby vertices (`merge_nearby_vertices`)

=== Behavior and constraints

`MergeResult merge_nearby_vertices(Sketch& sketch, double tolerance)` merges nearby non-construction line endpoints
within `tolerance` millimetres into shared topological `SketchPoint` entities:

* *Tolerance validation*: `tolerance` must be finite and in `(0, 1e6]` mm; otherwise `MergeStatus::kInvalidTolerance`
  is returned and `sketch` is unchanged.
* *Eligible vertices*: non-construction `SketchPoint` entities referenced as `start` or `end` of at least one
  non-construction `SketchLine`, excluding any point referenced as `center` of a `SketchCircle` or `SketchArc` so
  circle/arc centres are never collapsed into contour corners.
* *Deterministic clustering*: undirected edges connect eligible vertices `u` and `v` when
  `hypot(pos(u) - pos(v)) <= tolerance`. Each connected component of size `>= 2` merges into a single canonical
  surviving `EntityId`:
  ** A member with a `kFix` constraint has highest priority, then a contact bound by `kTangentStart`/`kTangentEnd`,
     then an ordinary endpoint, so fixed targets and bound contacts remain anchored; ties pick the lowest `EntityId`.
  ** Otherwise the lowest `EntityId` in the component survives.
* *Entity and constraint rebinding*:
  ** `SketchLine` endpoints are remapped to their cluster survivors. If remapping collapses any line (`start == end`),
     the operation is rejected with `MergeStatus::kCollapsedEntity` and `sketch` is unchanged.
  ** Point references in constraints (`kCoincident`, `kHorizontal`, `kVertical`, `kOnCurve`, `kFix`, `kDistance`) are
     remapped to survivors:
     *** Now-topological self-relations (`kCoincident(s, s)`, point-point `kHorizontal(s, s)` / `kVertical(s, s)`, and
         `kOnCurve(s, line)` when `s` becomes an endpoint of `line`) are removed.
     *** Exact duplicate constraints after remapping are deduplicated, retaining the lowest ID. Dimension values,
         reference flags and placement payloads remain significant; differing payloads are never silently dropped.
     *** Self point-point `kDistance(s, s)` or snapshot-invalid rebindings (such as merging both ends of one arc onto
         one point) reject with `MergeStatus::kConflict`.
* *Transactional solve*: the rebuilt `SketchSnapshot` is validated via `Sketch::from_snapshot` and solved with
  `solve(...)`. Only on `SolveStatus::kSolved` is `sketch` replaced (`MergeStatus::kMerged` with `merged_points > 0`).
  When no eligible pair lies within `tolerance`, `MergeStatus::kNothingToMerge` (`merged_points == 0`) leaves `sketch`
  unchanged without creating an Undo step.
* *UI and Undo*:
  ** Default merge tolerance in `SketchController` is `0.5` mm (retained across invocations in the session).
  ** A successful merge cancels any unfinished drawing/corner preview and clears stale selections, retaining the
     active tool. Failed or no-op merges preserve unfinished interaction state. Merging during a live document drag
     gesture is rejected without changing geometry or interaction state.
  ** Invoking “Объединить вершины…” from the overflow menu or tapping the open-gap status pill opens the numeric
     keypad (`DimensionEditor`) prefilled with the current tolerance; confirming runs one transactional `Document`
     command (`"Merge vertices"`) undoable in a single Undo/Redo step.

== Part 2: Closed profile validation and extraction (`analyze_profiles`)

=== Input filtering and node graph

`ProfileAnalysis analyze_profiles(const Sketch& sketch)` inspects `sketch` without mutating it:

* *Construction exclusion*: construction lines/arcs/circles and standalone helper points do not form contours,
  including chamfer/fillet virtual sharps. Normal curves still use their referenced point coordinates, including a
  construction-marked centre, so a fillet arc remains part of the contour.
* *Analytical curve primitives*:
  ** Non-construction `SketchLine` segments (`ProfileEdgeKind::kLine`).
  ** Non-construction `SketchArc` curves (`ProfileEdgeKind::kArc`) with exact `center`, `radius`, `start_angle`, and
     signed `sweep_angle`.
  ** Non-construction `SketchCircle` curves (`ProfileEdgeKind::kCircle`) with exact `center` and `radius`.
* *Endpoint coincidence (`kProfileEndpointTolerance = 1e-5` mm)*:
  ** Line and arc endpoints merge into contour graph nodes when they share a `SketchPoint`, are linked by `kCoincident`
     or `kTangentStart`/`kTangentEnd`, or lie within `1e-5` mm (well above the solver's `1e-7` mm length residual
     tolerance and well below unmerged user sketch gaps).
  ** Adjacent edges in a closed loop have their shared boundary `Position` unified to the node position so downstream
     3D wire builders receive exact matching vertex coordinates.

=== Validation diagnostics

* *Open endpoints (`open_endpoints`)*: every contour node of incident degree `1` is reported with its `Position`,
  optional `SketchPoint` ID, and incident curve ID. Open chains do not produce `ProfileFace` entries.
* *Invalid intersections and branching (`invalid_intersections`)*:
  ** Any contour node of degree `>= 3` (T-junction or shared boundary edge),
  ** Degenerate non-construction curves (zero-length segments or invalid curve bounds) block readiness instead of
     being silently ignored. Full circles use `SketchCircle`; an exact full-turn `SketchArc` is outside the current
     model contract (`abs(sweep) < 2*pi`) and is not newly enabled by this increment.
  ** Any non-endpoint crossing or overlap between two non-construction curves (line-line, line-arc, line-circle,
     arc-arc, arc-circle, circle-circle).
  ** Connected curve components participating in a branching node or curve crossing are excluded from `faces` and
     their offending positions are reported in `invalid_intersections`.

=== Loop orientation, exact area, and outer/hole nesting

* Each clean closed cycle (a standalone `SketchCircle` or a simple cycle of `degree == 2` nodes with no crossings)
  forms a `ProfileLoop` whose signed area is integrated analytically via Green's theorem over exact line segments and
  circular arcs (`+pi * r^2` for a CCW circle, exact circular-sector/chord terms for `SketchArc` fillets).
  Nonfinite or zero integrated areas are diagnosed and block readiness. Small finite positive areas are retained
  rather than discarded by an arbitrary fixed area cutoff, provided their endpoints pass the graph tolerances.
  Translate to a local integration origin to avoid cancellation at large world coordinates. Arc overlaps are tested
  analytically in angular intervals, so short overlaps between sampling positions also block readiness. Ignore only
  machine-roundoff-sized angular interval intersections at a shared seam; truly overlapping short arcs stay invalid.
* Containment between disjoint simple loops is decided by exact horizontal ray casting against analytical line segments,
  circular arcs, and circles.
* Loops at even containment depth (`0, 2, ...`) are oriented counter-clockwise (`signed_area > 0`) as outer boundaries
  of `ProfileFace` records; loops at odd containment depth (`1, 3, ...`) are oriented clockwise (`signed_area < 0`) and
  attached as `holes` of their immediate enclosing outer loop, with `face.area = outer.signed_area + sum(hole.signed_area)`.
* `ProfileAnalysis::ready_for_extrude()` is `true` iff `!faces.empty() && open_endpoints.empty() && invalid_intersections.empty()`.

== Canvas and shell presentation

* `SketchViewport` renders a subtle translucent fill (`profile_fill_path`, even-odd fill rule so holes stay clear)
  inside valid `ProfileFace` regions, and error-coloured rings (`open_endpoints_path`) around open contour endpoints
  and invalid crossings whenever no drawing tool gesture is in progress.
* The top bar displays a compact contour status pill (`profileStatus`) when the sketch contains non-construction curves:
  closed face/hole summary in `ok` green when `ready_for_extrude()`, or open-endpoint / crossing count in `err` red.
  Count open endpoints explicitly (“Открытых концов”), rather than calling two ends of one gap two gaps.
* Profile data and endpoint markers follow live geometry during selection drags and return to the committed geometry
  on cancellation. Cache invalidation must include live gestures, not only committed revision changes.
* At the minimum supported 720 px window width the contour status and merge action remain reachable without
  overlapping the other top-bar controls. Below 1150 px, elide the project text, abbreviate the DOF pill (full status
  remains in its tooltip) and hide the mode strip, whose other modes are currently disabled.
* `Help.qml` defines plain-language Russian explanations for `profiles` and `merge_vertices`.

== Acceptance criteria

* Merging an unclosed polyline/rectangle with gaps `<= tolerance` welds the endpoints into shared `SketchPoint` IDs,
  preserves driving/horizontal/vertical/chamfer/fillet constraints via `solve`, and reverts in one Undo step.
* Merging rejects non-positive/non-finite tolerances, sliver-line collapses (`tolerance >= segment length`), and
  conflicting fixed points without mutating the sketch or history.
* `analyze_profiles` extracts exact CCW outer loops and CW inner holes across rectangles, polygons, chamfered corners,
  filleted corners (`SketchArc` + construction virtual sharp), circular holes, oblong arc-line slot holes, multiple
  islands, and nested islands-in-holes, matching analytical areas to `< 1e-6` mm^2.
* Open endpoints and untrimmed crossings are reported accurately and highlighted in QML; closing gaps via
  “Объединить вершины…” clears open-endpoint markers and marks the profile ready.

== Explicit short-edge collapse (#60)

The merge keypad offers an unchecked option to collapse short non-construction line
edges. Default merge behavior remains unchanged. In explicit collapse mode, a line
whose endpoints map to one survivor is removed transactionally. Only simple chain
vertices (two incident non-construction lines, no arc bindings or construction
line incidence) may participate; branching or entire-loop erasure is rejected.

Axis constraints on a removed line become tautologies and are removed. On-curve
relations to an axis-aligned removed line become equivalent point-point axis
relations, retaining their IDs. Other relations to removed edges, including
positive driving length, tangency or parallelism, reject the whole operation.
Point distance dimensions involving collapsed endpoints become reference dimensions
with IDs and placement retained; the keypad explicitly states this consequence.
Fixed targets and all other driving constraints remain enforced. Unsupported
oblique on-curve conversion rejects. Solve must succeed before commit, and the
operation must not introduce new branching or intersections, or erase a closed
face. Success is one Undo step; failure changes neither geometry nor history.

Acceptance: rectangle 50x20, successive adjacent chamfers 10.51 and
9.48992 leaves a short side. Default merge rejects; explicit collapse with 0.5mm
removes that side and produces one valid closed five-edge face. All retained
curve IDs survive, affected dimensions become reference, and Undo restores the side.
Conflicting fixed targets, unsupported edge constraints and overlarge clusters
reject without mutation.
